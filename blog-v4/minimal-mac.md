# minimal mac setup


## Why we should seek minimalism?

1. to not get distracted
2. aesthetics
3. feeling organized

## how to setup minimal mac
1. hide dock
2. hide menu bar
3. aesthetic wallpaper
4. track todos with taskwarrior (todo app based on cli)
5. hide app with cmd+h or cmd+option+h when you are not using the app
6. use apple default apps ex) keynote=ppt, pages=word
7. use simple apps ex)discord+slack=ripcord
8. organize folders
9. nothing in desktop, or just one organized folder
10. do not use social network
11. format my mac once a month
